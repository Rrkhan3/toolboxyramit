Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Client-side processing

ToolBoxy tools run in your browser. Text, numbers and images you enter or select are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers by the tools.

Local storage

We store one item, your light or dark theme preference, in your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Cookies and analytics

ToolBoxy does not set its own cookies. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Advertising

ToolBoxy shows ads from third-party advertising networks. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, and may collect information such as your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. Ads are loaded in separate sandboxed frames and cannot read what you type into ToolBoxy tools. You can limit ad tracking through your browser settings or an ad blocker.

Cookies and similar technologies

ToolBoxy uses necessary local storage for theme and consent preferences. With your permission we may also use analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising-related technologies (Smartlink on certain download actions). Optional technologies load only after you consent via the cookie banner or Cookie Settings. Full details, categories, and how to withdraw consent are in our Cookie Policy.

Contact form

If the contact form is connected to an external form service, the details you submit (name, email, subject and message) are sent to that service so we can reply. Do not send sensitive information.

Third-party services

The site may use a hosting provider that keeps standard server logs (for example IP address and pages requested). Ad networks are third-party services as described above.

Contact

Questions? Use our contact page.
