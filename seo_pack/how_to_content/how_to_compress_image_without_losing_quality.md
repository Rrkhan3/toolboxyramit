## The Correct Way to Compress Without Visible Quality Loss

### 1. Resize First (Biggest Impact)
If your website displays the image at 800px width, don’t upload a 4000px version.  
Resize to the actual display size **before** compressing. This alone can cut size by 70–90%.

### 2. Choose the Right Format
| Use Case              | Best Format     | Reason                          |
|-----------------------|-----------------|---------------------------------|
| Photographs           | WebP or JPG     | Best size/quality balance       |
| Logos / Graphics      | PNG or WebP     | Needs sharp edges & transparency|
| Everything modern     | WebP            | 25–35% smaller than JPG         |

### 3. Use the Sweet-Spot Quality Setting
- **JPG / WebP photos**: Quality **75–85**
  - 80 is the practical sweet spot used by major platforms
  - Below 70 → visible artifacts
  - Above 90 → almost no visual gain, larger files
- **PNG logos**: Use lossless compression (or convert to WebP lossless)

### 4. Strip Metadata
Camera EXIF data (GPS, camera model, etc.) adds unnecessary bytes. Always remove it.
