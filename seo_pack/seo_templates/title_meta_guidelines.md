# Title & Meta Writing Guidelines for ToolBoxy

## Title Tag Formula (best performing)
Free [Tool Name] Online – [Benefit / Keyword] | ToolBoxy

Examples:
- Free Image Compressor Online – Compress Without Losing Quality | ToolBoxy
- Free EMI Calculator Online – Loan EMI Calculator | ToolBoxy
- Free JSON Formatter Online – Beautify & Validate JSON | ToolBoxy

Keep under 60 characters when possible.

## Meta Description Formula
[Action] [tool] [benefit]. Free online [tool], no signup, [privacy or speed point]. [CTA]

Examples:
- Compress images online for free without losing quality. Reduce JPG, PNG & WebP file size in your browser. No signup, private & fast.
- Calculate monthly loan EMI instantly. Free online EMI calculator for home, car and personal loans. Accurate results.

150-160 characters ideal.

## H1 Formula
Free [Tool Name] – [Main Benefit]

Keep it clear and keyword-rich. Only one H1 per page.

## Always Include These USPs
- Free
- No signup / No account
- Browser-based / Private (where true)
- Fast
- No watermark / No limits (if true)
