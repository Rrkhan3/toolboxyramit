Age Calculator

This free age calculator finds your exact age in years, months and days from a date of birth. Students use it for forms, parents track milestones, and anyone can check age on a chosen date. Enter birth date and optional “as of” date; the result appears immediately. Calculation runs entirely in your browser, so dates are not uploaded to a server.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Optionally set the “as of” date (defaults to today).
  3. Press Calculate to see years, months and days.
  4. Copy the result if you need it elsewhere.

How it works

The tool subtracts the birth date from the target date, borrowing days and months when needed so the result uses whole years, months and days.

Why use the Age Calculator

You get a precise breakdown without spreadsheets or mental math. Useful for official forms, party planning, or checking eligibility by age.

Common use cases

  • School or job application age fields
  • Birthday and milestone planning
  • Checking age eligibility for services
  • Comparing ages across family members

Example

Born on 15 March 2000, on 10 June 2025 the age is 25 years, 2 months and 26 days.

Age Calculator FAQ

Can I calculate age for a future date?

Yes. Set the “Age at the date of” field to any date after the birth date.

How is age calculated?

Age is the number of full years, months and days between the date of birth and the chosen date.

Does it account for leap years?

Yes. Calendar month lengths and leap days are handled by the date arithmetic.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. Dates stay in your browser for this session and are not sent to ToolBoxy’s servers by the tool.

Is the Age Calculator free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and runs in the browser.
