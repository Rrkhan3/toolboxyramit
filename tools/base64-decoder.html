Base64 Decoder

Paste Base64 and recover UTF-8 text when the payload is valid. Inspect encoded configs and samples locally.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Base64 Decoder

  1. Paste Base64.
  2. Press Decode.
  3. Copy the decoded text.

How it works

Base64 is decoded to bytes then interpreted as UTF-8; invalid input shows an error.

Why use the Base64 Decoder

Inspect encoded strings without a terminal.

Common use cases

  • Debugging tokens
  • Reading encoded samples
  • Classroom demos

Base64 Decoder FAQ

Why did decoding fail?

Input may include invalid characters, wrong padding, or non-UTF-8 bytes.
