BMI Calculator

The BMI calculator estimates body mass index from height and weight using the standard formula. It is a screening number, not a medical diagnosis. Fitness tracking and general awareness are common uses. Values are processed in the browser only.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the BMI Calculator

  1. Enter height and weight in the units shown.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Read the BMI value and category label if shown.

How it works

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)² (or equivalent from other units). Categories follow common WHO-style ranges for adults.

Why use the BMI Calculator

A quick reference number for health discussions with a professional—not a substitute for clinical advice.

Common use cases

  • Personal fitness tracking
  • School or workplace health forms
  • Comparing trends over time with a clinician

BMI Calculator FAQ

Is BMI the same for everyone?

BMI is a population screening tool. Muscle mass, age and other factors affect interpretation.

Is this medical advice?

No. Use it as information only and consult a qualified professional for health decisions.

Are my measurements uploaded?

No. Height and weight stay in your browser for the calculation.
