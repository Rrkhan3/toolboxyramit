Color Picker & Converter

Choose a color visually or type HEX/RGB/HSL and convert between formats. Designers and developers match brand colors quickly. Local conversion.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Color Picker & Converter

  1. Pick a color or enter HEX, RGB or HSL.
  2. Apply the values.
  3. Copy HEX or other formats.

How it works

Conversions use standard RGB↔HSL mathematics and hex parsing.

Why use the Color Picker & Converter

Consistent colors across CSS and design tools.

Common use cases

  • CSS theming
  • Brand palette checks
  • Design handoff

Color Picker & Converter FAQ

Are colors color-managed?

Values are standard sRGB-style numbers in the browser, not a full CMS profile workflow.
