Compound Interest Calculator

Compound interest adds interest to the balance so growth accelerates over time. Enter principal, rate, time and compounding frequency to estimate future value. Savers and investors use it for rough projections. Runs in the browser.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Compound Interest Calculator

  1. Enter principal, annual rate and time.
  2. Select compounding frequency if available.
  3. Press Calculate.
  4. Review future value and interest earned.

How it works

Future value uses (1 + r/n)^(n·t) style growth on the principal for the chosen compounding schedule.

Why use the Compound Interest Calculator

Visualize long-term savings or investment growth without a spreadsheet.

Common use cases

  • Savings goal estimates
  • Comparing compounding schedules
  • Teaching compound growth

Compound Interest Calculator FAQ

Does this include deposits over time?

This tool focuses on a single principal; regular deposits need a different formula.

Is past performance guaranteed?

No. This is pure mathematics on the numbers you enter.

Is it free?

Yes.
