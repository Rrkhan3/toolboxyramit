Free Discount Calculator – Calculate Sale Price

This discount calculator shows the final price and amount saved when a percentage off is applied. Shoppers compare deals; sellers verify promo math. Enter original price and discount rate—results appear in the browser with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Press Calculate.
  4. Review final price and savings.

How it works

Final price = original × (1 − discount/100). Savings = original − final price.

Why use the Discount Calculator

Know exactly what you pay and save before checkout, without mental math errors.

Common use cases

  • Online and store sale shopping
  • Pricing promotions for small shops
  • Comparing multi-item discounts
  • Budget planning for purchases

Discount Calculator FAQ

Can I use a fixed amount off instead of percent?

This tool focuses on percentage discounts. For fixed amounts, subtract manually or use a general calculator.

Does it include tax?

No. It applies only the discount to the price you enter.

Is the Discount Calculator free?

Yes.
