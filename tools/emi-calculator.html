EMI Calculator

Plan loans with this EMI calculator: monthly installment from principal, annual rate and tenure. Useful for personal loans, vehicle finance and rough mortgage estimates. Math runs in the browser so loan figures are not submitted to a server by the tool.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the EMI Calculator

  1. Enter loan amount (principal).
  2. Enter annual interest rate and tenure.
  3. Press Calculate.
  4. Review EMI, total payment and interest.

How it works

EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula based on monthly rate and number of installments.

Why use the EMI Calculator

Estimate affordability before talking to a lender or comparing offers.

Common use cases

  • Personal and auto loan planning
  • Comparing lender quotes
  • Budgeting monthly repayments

EMI Calculator FAQ

Is this the same as bank EMI?

It matches the common mathematical formula; banks may add fees or use different day-count conventions.

Can I change tenure unit?

Use the fields as labeled (years/months depending on the form).

Is the EMI Calculator free?

Yes.
