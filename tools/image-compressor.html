Image Compressor FAQ

Can you compress images without losing quality?

Yes. Lossless compression keeps every pixel identical (usually 10-30% smaller). Lossy compression at quality 75-85 looks virtually identical to the human eye while reducing file size by 50-80%.

What is the best quality setting for web images?

For photographs use quality 75-85 (80 is the sweet spot). For logos and graphics with few colors use lossless compression.

Is the ToolBoxy Image Compressor free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no limits, no watermark.

Are my images uploaded to your servers?

No. ToolBoxy is designed so supported image tools process data in your browser. Your files stay on your device.

Does compressing images improve SEO?

Yes. Smaller images load faster, which improves Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) — one of Google’s Core Web Vitals ranking factors.
