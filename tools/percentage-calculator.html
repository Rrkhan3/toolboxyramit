Percentage Calculator FAQ

What is percent change?

Percent change measures how much a value rose or fell relative to its starting amount.

Yes—use the fields that ask for the part and the whole, or percent of a number, depending on the mode.

Does it handle decimals?

Yes. Enter decimal values when needed.
