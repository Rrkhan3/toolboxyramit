Percentage Calculator

Use this percentage calculator to answer everyday percent questions: what is X% of Y, what percent is A of B, or how much something increased or decreased. Students, shoppers and analysts get clear results without a spreadsheet. All math runs locally in the browser.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Enter the values for the calculation you need.
  2. Choose the operation (percent of, percent change, etc.) if shown.
  3. Press Calculate.
  4. Read the result cards and copy if needed.

How it works

Percentages are computed with standard arithmetic (part ÷ whole × 100, or relative change formulas) and formatted for readability.

Why use the Percentage Calculator

Avoid calculator apps or long formulas when you need a quick percent answer for homework, discounts or reports.

Common use cases

  • Homework and exam practice
  • Sale and discount checks
  • Business growth or decline rates
  • Tip and tax estimates

Percentage Calculator FAQ

What is percent change?

Percent change measures how much a value rose or fell relative to its starting amount.

Can I calculate reverse percentages?

Yes—use the fields that ask for the part and the whole, or percent of a number, depending on the mode.

Does it handle decimals?

Yes. Enter decimal values when needed.

Is the Percentage Calculator free?

Yes. Free to use with no account.
