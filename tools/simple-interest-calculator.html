Simple Interest Calculator

Simple interest grows only on the original principal. Enter principal, annual rate and time to get interest and total amount. Students and small lenders use it for basic interest problems. Computation is local in the browser.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Simple Interest Calculator

  1. Enter principal, rate and time.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Read interest and total amount.

How it works

Interest = principal × rate × time (with rate as a fraction per year and time in years, adjusted if needed).

Why use the Simple Interest Calculator

Clear answers for homework and short-term lending without compound complexity.

Common use cases

  • School interest problems
  • Short-term personal loans
  • Quick what-if scenarios

Simple Interest Calculator FAQ

How is this different from compound interest?

Simple interest does not earn interest on previous interest; compound does.

Can time be in months?

Convert months to years (months ÷ 12) if the form expects years, or use the unit shown.

Is it free?

Yes.
