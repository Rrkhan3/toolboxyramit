Slug Generator

Turn a title into a URL-friendly slug: lowercase, accents stripped, spaces to hyphens. Bloggers and developers prepare paths quickly. Local only.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Slug Generator

  1. Enter a title or phrase.
  2. See the slug update (live or on action).
  3. Copy the slug into your CMS or code.

How it works

Text is normalized (NFD), non-ASCII marks removed, lowercased, and non-alphanumeric runs replaced with hyphens.

Why use the Slug Generator

Consistent, readable URLs without manual cleanup.

Common use cases

  • Blog post URLs
  • Documentation paths
  • Product page handles

Slug Generator FAQ

Are accents removed?

Yes, common diacritics are stripped for ASCII-safe slugs.

Is it free?

Yes.
