Word Counter

Paste text to count words, characters (with and without spaces), lines, paragraphs and approximate reading time. Writers, students and editors check limits for essays, tweets and briefs. Counting runs live in the browser—text is not uploaded by the tool.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the Word Counter

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. Watch counts update live.
  3. Copy results if you need them in another app.

How it works

Words are split on whitespace; characters use Unicode code points; reading time assumes about 200 words per minute.

Why use the Word Counter

Stay within assignment, SEO or social limits without third-party paste sites.

Common use cases

  • Essay and assignment limits
  • Blog and SEO drafts
  • Social post length checks

Word Counter FAQ

Does it count hyphenated words as one?

Yes—hyphenated tokens without spaces typically count as one word.

Are spaces counted in characters?

Yes, and a separate figure excludes spaces.

Is my text stored?

No. Text stays in your browser session for counting.
